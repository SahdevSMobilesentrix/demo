Smart Money OI Strategy — Overview

We track NIFTY weekly options every minute and watch where institutional writers (the "smart money") are adding or removing positions. When they sell puts aggressively, support is being built → Bullish. When they sell calls aggressively, resistance is being built → Bearish. No clear pressure → Neutral.

UniverseNIFTY weekly · 10 strikes (5 below ATM + ATM + 4 above)
Cadence1-minute OI snapshots · ΔOI vs ~15 min ago
Signal window09:45 – 14:00 IST (avoids open/close noise)
Risk gateSkipped if India VIX moves > 4% intraday

How it Works

  1. Collect Call & Put OI for the 10 nearest strikes once per minute.
  2. Compute ΔOI for each strike vs the snapshot ~15 minutes earlier.
  3. Score each candidate strike on 4 checks (need 3 of 4 to qualify):
    • Relative OI change is large vs that strike's recent baseline
    • Volume / OI ratio is low (writing, not churn)
    • Direction matches writing (not unwinding)
    • Cumulative OI trend across the day agrees
  4. Pick side: Put writing at-or-below spot → Bullish · Call writing at-or-above spot → Bearish.
  5. Confirm with PCR & net OI flow, then publish the signal with a confidence %.

Worked Example

Spot 23 715 · ATM 23 700. Over the last 15 minutes Put OI at 23 700 / 23 650 jumped +14.94 L while Call OI at 23 750 / 23 800 fell −14.24 L. PCR rose to 1.34, net flow +29.17 L.

Read: writers are selling puts below spot (support being built) and covering calls above spot (resistance breaking). All 4 checks pass on the 23 700 PE → engine emits Strong Bullish at ~100% confidence. Paper trader would buy ATM CE; stop = invalidation strike, target = next OI wall.

Inverse case for a bearish signal: rising Call OI above spot + falling Put OI below spot.
